The plant problems model (downloaded when you ask) was trained on BioCAP's view of the photos below. No photo is
included in the app or the model. Each photo used is listed, with its credit and licence, in credits.tsv inside the
downloaded pack.

PlantDoc
Davinder Singh, Naman Jain, Pranjali Jain, Pratik Kayal, Sudhakar Kumawat and Nipun Batra. PlantDoc: A Dataset for
Visual Plant Disease Detection. Proceedings of the 7th ACM IKDD CoDS and 25th COMAD (CoDS-COMAD 2020), pages 249 to
253. https://doi.org/10.1145/3371158.3371196
https://github.com/pratikkayal/PlantDoc-Dataset
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

PlantVillage
Sharada P. Mohanty, David P. Hughes and Marcel Salathé. Using deep learning for image-based plant disease detection.
Frontiers in Plant Science 7 (2016). https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2016.01419
https://github.com/spMohanty/PlantVillage-Dataset
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (CC BY-SA 3.0), as its dataset card states,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

iNaturalist
Research-grade observations on iNaturalist (https://www.inaturalist.org) whose photos their observers released under
CC0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) or Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0). Each
observer is credited in the pack's credits.tsv.
