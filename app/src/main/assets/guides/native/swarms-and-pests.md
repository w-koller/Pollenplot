## Swarms

A swarm at a stingless bee hive is nothing to be overly concerned about. There are three kinds, and each looks different.

- **Fighting swarm.** Bees from another colony try to take the hive over, gripping its bees and fighting to the death, which leaves a mass of dead bees on the ground in front of the entrance. Fighting swarms mostly happen in summer and can last a few weeks. The hive usually survives, even if the other colony takes it over.
- **Defending swarm.** The hive keeps out bees from another hive trying to get in. It mostly happens when hives are moved or kept close together, leaves no dead bees on the ground, and lasts a few days to a few weeks.
- **Mating swarm.** The hive needs a new queen. Many males fly gently around it for days or weeks, waiting for her to come out and mate. It is less aggressive than a fighting swarm.

## Pests in the hive

A strong hive keeps most pests from doing harm, but a few can destroy one.

- **Syrphid fly**, a hover fly that looks like a wasp, lays its eggs through open joints and gaps, most often in a hive weakened by a split. Please seal the gaps with tape, and replace the tape when it needs it.
- **Phorid fly** gets in through any opening, lays its eggs inside and can destroy a hive within days. Broken honey and pollen pots attract it, so please avoid opening a hive. If you also keep honey bees, please keep the area clean and don't leave honey lying around.
- **Small hive beetle** is far more common in honey bee hives, but it does get into stingless bee hives. Good housekeeping around any honey bee hives lowers the risk.

## Around the hive

- **Bembix wasps** hover outside the hive and catch bees as they fly out.
- **Ants** are common around hives, especially log hives, and seldom harm the bees. To keep them away, please stand the hive off the ground on a post and grease it, or put a water barrier around it.
- **Spiders** can be left alone, since they eat pest insects as well as a few bees, but please keep their webs away from the entrance.
- **Black soldier flies** can attack a hive near compost or a worm farm, so please keep hives away from them.
- **Assassin bugs** take only a few bees.
- **Other bees** mostly take a little resin from the entrance, but many honey bees can rob a hive of everything.
