## Parasites

- **Cuckoo bees** sneak into blue-banded bee nests left unattended and lay their eggs, and their young eat the food stored for the host's young. Others, *Coelioxys*, slip into leafcutter bee nests. Aussie Bee is reluctant to remove them from the garden.
- **Gasteruptiid wasps** attack resin bee nests. Aussie Bee catches them with a net and removes them.
- **Tiny parasitic wasps**, such as *Melittobia*, are less than 2 mm long, too small to catch with a net. Cleaning out the nest material now and then keeps them down.
- **Mites** build up in a hotel, feeding on pollen and the nests' contents. Cleaning out the nest material now and then is the best defence against them too.

## Spiders and ants

- Spiders may catch adult bees using the hotel, so please look over it now and then and brush away any webs.
- Ants are drawn to the contents of the nest cells. To keep them off, please smear Vaseline or a horticultural glue that is not toxic around the hotel's support.

## Cleaning nests

Cleaning out nest holes directly can kill young bees still developing in them, so Aussie Bee uses an emergence box and waits for the bees to come out.

1. Once the bees have come out, put the nests in a freezer for a week, to kill any mites or parasites left in them.
2. Let the nests come back to room temperature, and put them back in the hotel to use again.

Paper lining tubes, put inside each drilled hole or bamboo tube before the bees move in, make this easier. In spring, after the bees have come out, please take out the old liner and any nest debris, and put in a new liner.
